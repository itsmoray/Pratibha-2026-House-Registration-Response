Part II · Registration instructions

  • A maximum of five (5) events — including group events — can be registered per student, in one of two combinations: 3 off-stage + 2 on-stage, or 2 off-stage + 3 on-stage. This limit doesn't apply to Short Film.
  • Students can register for a maximum of two on-stage group events.
  • Important: for each house, only two teams may register for any particular group event. If more than two register, the first two registered are accepted.
  • Only the team leader should register a group event; they list the other members. Multiple entries for the same team are grounds for disqualification.
  • All group and individual events (except off-stage literary events) must be registered only after consulting your house's faculty/student coordinators.
  • Do not select more than 5 events — any extra event registered will be cancelled without prior notice.
  • Coordinators have the final say on merging or cancelling events based on participation.

Part III · Event rules

Musical events (PAF 01–11)

PAF 01Light Music Vocal (Indian) — Solo — 5 min

  • Any Indian language.
  • Film songs, classical/semi-classical songs and karaoke are not allowed.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 02Classical Music Vocal — Solo — 10 min

  • Hindustani or Carnatic style.
  • Cinema songs not allowed; choose raga and composition carefully.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 03Western Vocal — Solo — 5 min

  • Song must be in English.
  • A pre-recorded instrumental/karaoke track may be used, but with no vocals on the track; submit as MP3 in advance.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 04Group Song (Indian) — 10 min (6–8 members)

  • Only 2 entries per house.
  • Two songs performed, one of which must be patriotic; any Indian language.
  • No film songs.
  • 2–3 instruments (non-singers) allowed; singers may also use more instruments.
  • 2 minutes setup time per house, separate from the 10-minute slot.

PAF 05Group Song (Western) — 10 min (6–8 members)

  • Performance in English; any Western genre (pop, rock, gospel, acapella).
  • Acapella, or accompanied by up to 3 instruments played by members/accompanists — no pre-recorded tracks.
  • Only 2 entries per house.
  • 2–3 instruments (non-singers) allowed; singers may also use more.
  • 2 minutes setup time per house, separate from the 10-minute slot.
  • Judged on quality of singing only, not makeup.

PAF 06Stringed Instrument (Eastern) — 10 min (Individual)

  • Bring your own manual instrument; must perform with sruti.
  • Western instruments adapted to Indian raga system allowed.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 07Stringed Instrument (Western) — 10 min (Individual)

  • Bring your own manual instrument.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 08Wind Instrument (Eastern) — 10 min (Individual)

  • Indian wind instruments, or Western instruments adapted to the Indian raga system (harmonium, flute, pullankuzhal, bansuri, nadhaswaram, etc.).
  • Bring your own manual instrument.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 09Wind Instrument (Western) — 10 min (Individual)

  • Western wind instruments (harmonium, accordion, clarinet, saxophone, organ, etc.), presented in Western styles.
  • Electronically operated keyboards don't count as wind instruments.
  • Bring your own instrument. Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 10Percussion Instrument (Eastern) — 10 min (Individual)

  • Carnatic or Hindustani style; bring your own instrument.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 11Percussion Instrument (Western) — 10 min (Individual)

  • Western style; bring your own instrument.
  • Only 2 participants per house.
Dance events (PAF 12–15)

PAF 12Classical Dance — Solo — 10 min

  • Any approved classical Indian genre (Kathakali, Bharatanatyam, Mohiniyattam, Kuchippudi, Kathak, Manipuri, Odissi, etc.).
  • Judged on talam, abhinayam, footwork and general impression — costume earns no extra marks, but should be traditional and appropriate.
  • Share the song with the media/sound team at registration.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 13Western Dance — Solo — 5 min

  • Costume, makeup and props should be authentic style — no extra marks for costume.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 14Folk Dance — Group — 8 min (6–8 members)

  • Authentic folk dance from any Indian region; folk music only, no classical form.
  • Costume, makeup and props must be authentic; share the song with the media/sound team at registration.
  • Judged on rhythm, formations, costume/makeup and overall effect.
  • Teams are responsible for removing their own sets/props.
  • Only 2 entries per house.

PAF 15Group Dance (Classical/Semi-Classical/Western/Contemporary) — 8 min (6–8 members)

  • Choreography should show coordinated movement, formations and expression.
  • Share the song with the media/sound team at registration.
  • Teams are responsible for removing their own sets/props.
  • Only 2 entries per house.
Literary events (PAF 16–37)

PAF 16–19Elocution — Malayalam / English / Hindi / Sanskrit — 5 min (Individual)

  • Only 3 participants per house, per language.
  • Topic given 15 minutes before your turn — no books, phones, laptops or written scripts after that.
  • Must be delivered in the event's language.

PAF 20–23Essay Writing — Malayalam / English / Hindi / Sanskrit — 1 hour (Individual)

  • Topic given at the start; 800–1000 words (roughly 4–5 pages), handwritten on the sheets provided, in the event's language only.
  • Neat, legible handwriting; no extra time.
  • Only blue/black pens for writing (pencil for underlining only); no reference material, books or phones.

PAF 24–27Poetry Writing — Malayalam / English / Hindi / Sanskrit — 1 hour (Individual)

  • Topic/theme announced at the venue; must be an original composition written during the competition.
  • 12–30 lines (max 2 pages), in the event's language only, on the sheets provided.
  • Only blue/black pens for writing (pencil for underlining/titles only); no books, notes or phones.

PAF 28–31Story Writing — Malayalam / English / Hindi / Sanskrit — 1 hour (Individual)

  • Prompt given at the start; must be an original work of fiction, 600–800 words (roughly 3–4 pages).
  • Handwritten on the sheets provided, only blue/black pens; no reference material or electronic devices.

PAF 32–35Recitation — Malayalam / English / Hindi / Sanskrit — 5 min (Individual)

  • Only 3 participants per house, per language.
  • Only published literary works in the event's language, recited from memory — no reading or prompting.
  • Voice modulation, pronunciation and clarity matter; avoid unnecessary dramatization or singing, and keep gestures natural.

PAF 36Geetha Chanting — 5 min (Individual)

  • Only 3 participants per house.
  • Any verse(s) from the Bhagavad Gita, chanted from memory in the original Sanskrit — no books or reference material.
  • Correct pronunciation, rhythm, pitch and traditional chanting style expected; no dramatization or singing.

PAF 37Debate (English) — 10 min (5 per team)

  • Only one team per house.
  • Topic announced 30 minutes in advance; stance ('For'/'Against') decided by lot at the venue.
  • Each speaker gets 5 minutes, followed by a 2-minute rebuttal.
  • Cue cards allowed, full scripts discouraged; no abusive language, personal attacks or irrelevant points.
  • Malpractice or disruptive behaviour leads to disqualification.
Short film (PAF 38)

PAF 38Short Film — under 2 minutes

  • Theme: "The Rhythm of Sapta Swaras". Submit as .MP4/.MOV, Full HD (1920×1080), via the specified link or pen drive.
  • Must be original and not previously published or screened publicly; content must be original, royalty-free, or permission obtained — no obscene, defamatory or offensive material.
  • Any language accepted; non-English/Malayalam films need English subtitles.
  • Cast size can be based on the storyline; only one certificate is issued for the director & team.
  • Not counted against the usual 5-event / off-stage-on-stage limit.
  • Submission deadline: 28th September, before 5:00 pm.
Theatre events (PAF 39–42)

PAF 39Monoact — 5 min (Individual)

  • Dramatic solo performance; original or adapted theme/script, in English, Malayalam or Hindi.
  • Costumes/light makeup and minimal props allowed; no background music or sound effects.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 40Mimicry — 5 min (Individual)

  • Imitate voices/sounds of personalities, animals or objects; present a variety of items.
  • Content must be decent — no offensive/vulgar material; no background music.
  • Only 2 participants per house.

PAF 41Mime — 6 min (3–6 members)

  • Convey a story/theme through gesture, expression and body language — no lip movement, speech or sound.
  • Light instrumental music allowed (no lyrics); minimal props, appropriate costume, face painting allowed.
  • Only 2 teams per house.

PAF 42Fancy Dress — 2–3 min (Individual)

  • Choose any character — historical, cultural, social, mythological or creative — that's decent and non-offensive.
  • Arrange your own costume, props and makeup; props must be manageable and safe — no fire, water or sharp/dangerous objects.
  • A short dialogue/verse/act related to the character is fine, but only your own voice — no recordings, background music or prompting.
  • Only 2 participants per house.
Fine arts events (PAF 43–49)

PAF 43Water Colouring — 2 hours (Individual)

  • Topic given at the venue; A3 sheet provided.
  • Bring your own watercolours, brushes, palette and tools — watercolour medium only.
  • Only 4 participants per house.

PAF 44Pencil Drawing — 2 hours (Individual)

  • Topic announced at the venue.
  • Bring your own pencils, erasers, sharpeners and drawing sheets.
  • Only 4 participants per house.

PAF 45Clay Modelling — 2 hours (Individual)

  • Topic announced on the spot; clay provided, but you may bring your own tools.
  • No external molds, colours or decorative materials.
  • Only 4 participants per house.

PAF 46Rangoli — 2 hours (2 per team)

  • Topic announced on the spot; 1×1 m space per participant.
  • Bring your own materials (coloured powders, sawdust, rice flour, flower petals) — no stencils, stickers or ready-made patterns.
  • Only one Rangoli per house.

PAF 47Photography — 7 hours, 10:00 am–4:00 pm on the 30th (Individual)

  • Theme given at the start; photos must be taken on campus/the designated area within the allotted time.
  • Mobile phones only — no DSLR or other cameras.
  • Submit one JPEG, no editing/post-processing/watermarks.
  • The university retains all rights to use the submitted pictures.

PAF 48Collage — 2 hours (2 per team)

  • Topic given on the spot; A3 base sheet provided.
  • Bring newspaper/magazine cuttings, scissors, adhesive, pencils, erasers, pens — no paint, sketches or other decorative materials.
  • Only 2 teams per house.

PAF 49Mehendi Design — 2 hours (2 per team)

  • Bring your own model and Mehendi cones (natural preferred); theme (Bridal/Festive/Arabic) given on the spot.
  • No stencils, glitter or other decorative items.
  • Design only on both lower arms — palmer side of both hands, extendable to the palmer forearm with a minimum 6-inch length.
  • Only 2 teams per house.
